Privacy Policy

Bushido Academy is developed by dacape.dev. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using the app.

What Bushido Academy Is

Bushido Academy is an educational tool for learning stock market analysis concepts. It does not provide financial advice, does not connect to any broker, and does not execute trades. All stock data (ticker, prices, indicator readings) is entered manually by the user based on what they observe on their own charting platform.

Information Collection and Use

Bushido Academy does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information. The stock analyses and positions you save in the app (ticker, prices, dates, checklist results) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, including:

  • Google AdMob (advertising services, for non-Pro users)
  • Google Play Billing (processing of the optional Pro one-time purchase)
  • Google User Messaging Platform / UMP (consent management for ads)

These services may collect device information such as device type, operating system version, advertising identifiers, usage data, and IP address. This data is used to provide ads, manage ad consent, and process optional in-app purchases. Purchase and payment details are handled entirely by Google Play Billing — Bushido Academy never receives or stores your payment information.

Third-Party Service Providers

Bushido Academy uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Terms
  • Google Play Billing Terms

We do not sell personal data. Data collected by third-party services is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

Advertising

Bushido Academy displays ads provided by Google AdMob to non-Pro users. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and cookies to show relevant ads based on user activity. Users can reset or limit ad tracking through their device settings, and can remove ads entirely by purchasing the optional Pro upgrade.

Data Security

We value your trust in providing information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Bushido Academy is not intended for children under 16 years of age, due to advertising and third-party data collection (AdMob), and because its content (stock market analysis education) is aimed at adult learners.

Not Financial Advice

Bushido Academy is an educational tool for practicing analysis. It does not provide financial advice or personalized investment recommendations. Investing carries the risk of capital loss. Decisions about your money are always yours.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
